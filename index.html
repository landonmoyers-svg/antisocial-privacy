Antisocial Browser: Privacy Policy

Effective October 28, 2026

Antisocial Browser is a reader mode for video: it opens video links you choose and shows just the video. It is built to keep everything on your iPhone.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The developer runs no servers and receives no data from the app.

What stays on your iPhone

  • The links you save, their titles, and a small blurred preview image for each.
  • If you use the Messages automation or Share, the name and phone number or email of whoever sent a link, so you can reply. This is stored only in the app on your phone.
  • Your settings, such as which content filters are on.

Content filters run entirely on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes all of this.

Websites you open

When you play a saved link, the app loads that website the way Safari would, using Apple's WebKit. That website may set cookies or collect data under its own privacy policy, just as it would in a browser. The app does not share anything about you with those sites beyond what loading the page requires.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.
